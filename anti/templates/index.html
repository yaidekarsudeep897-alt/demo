Core Citizen Service Categories

SmartGram provides dedicated reporting and monitoring channels across essential rural public service domains.

🏡

Village Problems

Roads, streetlights, drainage, water supply, waste management, and other local infrastructure issues.

  • Potholes & damaged link roads
  • Dark spots & broken solar lights
  • Drinking water pipeline bursts & handpump silt
  • Clogged open drains & waste disposal
Report Village Problem →
🏫

School Problems

School facilities, classrooms, sanitation, drinking water, infrastructure, and midday-meal quality.

  • Separate toilets for girls & boys
  • Safe drinking water & filtration
  • Daily Midday Meal (MDM) quality feedback
  • Classroom roof leaks, desks & electricity
Report School Issue →
🩺

Health Problems

Local health-service concerns, medicine availability, PHC staffing, and public health camp registration.

  • Doctor & ANM availability at sub-centre
  • Essential medicines & anti-venom supply
  • Infant immunization & maternal care
  • Free mega health camp appointment slips
Report Health Concern →
